# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to other people. Technical disagreement is welcome and often the point
of a review; contempt for the person on the other side of it is not.

## What is expected

- Argue about code, data and evidence, not about people.
- Accept that reviewers ask for changes, and that maintainers say no. Both are
  normal parts of the process.
- Assume the other person is working in good faith, especially across a language
  barrier. Contributors here work in several languages and most regulatory
  sources are not in English.
- Say when you are unsure. A question is cheaper than a wrong regulatory
  deadline reaching someone who acts on it.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults, or discriminatory remarks of any kind. Sustained disruption
of discussions. Publishing anyone's private information. Sexualised language or
attention. Deliberately submitting code or data you believe to be wrong.

## Enforcement

The maintainer will remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and pull
requests that break these rules, and will explain why when doing so. Repeated or
serious breaches lead to a ban from the project.

Report a problem to julian.laycock@caelith.tech. Reports are handled privately.
If a report concerns the maintainer, you are entitled to raise it publicly in an
issue instead, and it will not be deleted.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, in issues and pull requests, and anywhere
somebody is representing the project in public.

This document is a plain-language equivalent of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
which is the reference text if anything here is ambiguous.
